Rabbit Stew

Home

Description

In Bedrock Edition, novice-level Butcher villagers always offer to sell rabbit stew for one emerald as their second trade.

In Java Edition, novice-level Butcher villagers have a 50% chance of offering rabbit stew for one emerald.

Ingredients

  • Cooked Rabbit
  • Carrot
  • Baked Potato
  • Any Mushroom
  • Bowl

How To Make

  1. Harvest and cook the rabbit using a furnace.
  2. Craft together the ingredients using a crafting table.
  3. To eat rabbit stew, press and hold use while it is selected in the hotbar.
